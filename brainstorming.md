## New App Ideas - Evaluate and Categorize

### CineStream

**Description:** CineStream helps movie and television enthusiasts discover trending titles and view useful metadata, such as a title's synopsis, release date, rating, genres, cast, and poster.

**Category:** Entertainment / Media Discovery

**Mobile:** CineStream is well suited to mobile because users often decide what to watch while away from a computer or while relaxing at home. A compact, scrollable feed and tap-to-view details make discovery quick on a phone.

**Story:** The app solves the familiar problem of spending too much time deciding what to watch. It gives users an easy way to spot popular titles and understand what each one is about before choosing.

**Market:** Movie and TV viewers form a large audience, and the app has a clear niche among people who want a simpler discovery experience without opening multiple streaming services.

**Habit:** Trending content changes frequently, so users can return regularly to see what is popular and explore newly released titles.

**Scope:** A focused first version can display trending movies and TV shows, support title search, and show a details screen with metadata from one movie database API. Watchlists, personalized recommendations, and streaming-provider availability can remain future enhancements.

### CampusBytes

**Description:** CampusBytes helps students discover campus events based on their interests, available time, and location.

**Category:** Education / Lifestyle

**Mobile:** Event notifications, maps, and on-the-go browsing make a mobile app useful when students are moving between classes.

**Story:** Students often miss activities because event information is scattered across flyers, email, and social media. CampusBytes creates one convenient place to find them.

**Market:** The primary audience is students at a college or university. The app could be adapted for many campuses, but the initial audience is narrower than a general entertainment app.

**Habit:** Students may check the app weekly or before weekends, with notifications encouraging repeat use when relevant events are added.

**Scope:** A first version could list events, offer filtering by date and category, and show event details. Reliable campus-event data and account management could add complexity.

### ShelfSwap

**Description:** ShelfSwap enables students to find, list, and exchange used textbooks and course supplies locally.

**Category:** Marketplace / Productivity

**Mobile:** Students can photograph books, create listings, browse nearby items, and message sellers from their phones, making mobile a natural platform.

**Story:** The app helps students save money and reduce waste by making it easier to reuse materials within their school community.

**Market:** College students buy expensive materials every term, creating a clear audience. The product is especially useful when it has enough local users at a participating campus.

**Habit:** Use would peak around the beginning and end of academic terms rather than being a daily habit.

**Scope:** Listings, photos, search, and basic contact features are achievable, but user accounts, moderation, and transaction safety would make a full marketplace more complex than the milestone timeline allows.

## Final App Idea

After evaluating the finalists, the team voted to build **CineStream**. It has a broad audience, a clear mobile experience, frequently refreshed trending content, and a practical scope for a first Android release. The initial product will focus on discovering trending movies and TV shows and viewing movie metadata.
